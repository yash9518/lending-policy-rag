MERIDIAN RETAIL FINANCE LTD. (FICTIONAL) - KYC AND DOCUMENTATION POLICY (v3.0)

1. CUSTOMER IDENTIFICATION:
- PAN (or Form 60 where PAN is not available) is mandatory for every borrower and co-applicant.
- Proof of identity and address must be an Officially Valid Document: Aadhaar (with consent, masked to the last 4 digits when stored), passport, voter ID, or driving licence.
- Video-based Customer Identification Process (V-CIP) is permitted when conducted live by a trained official with geo-tagging and a recorded session.
- KYC records must be uploaded to the Central KYC Records Registry (CKYCR) within 10 days of commencement of the account-based relationship.

2. INCOME DOCUMENTATION:
- Salaried applicants: salary slips for the last 3 months and bank statements for the last 6 months showing salary credits.
- Self-employed applicants: income tax returns for the last 2 years with computation of income, and bank statements for the last 12 months.
- Self-employed applicants with turnover above INR 1 crore must also submit audited financial statements and GST returns for the last 4 quarters.
- Net monthly income for FOIR is computed after statutory deductions; variable pay is considered at 50% of the 12-month average.

3. PROPERTY AND COLLATERAL DOCUMENTS:
- Housing and secured business loans require a legal title report covering at least 13 years of title chain and a technical valuation by an empanelled valuer.
- Where two valuations differ by more than 15%, the lower valuation is used for LTV computation.
- Original title documents are held in safe custody and logged in the document management register.

4. RE-KYC AND RISK CATEGORISATION:
- Customers are risk-categorised as low, medium or high at onboarding.
- Periodic KYC update is required every 2 years for high-risk, every 8 years for medium-risk, and every 10 years for low-risk customers.
- Politically exposed persons are always classified as high risk and require senior management approval.
