MERIDIAN RETAIL FINANCE LTD. (FICTIONAL) - UNDERWRITING POLICY GUIDELINES (CREDIT POLICY v4.2)

1. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Minimum Age: 21 years at the time of loan application; Maximum Age: 60 years at loan maturity.
- Minimum Net Monthly Income: INR 25,000 for salaried employees; INR 40,000 for self-employed professionals.
- Maximum Debt-to-Income (DTI) or Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR): 50% for income < INR 50,000/month; up to 65% for income >= INR 50,000/month.

2. LOAN SIZES AND TENURES:
- Personal Loans: INR 50,000 to INR 10,00,000. Tenure: 12 to 60 months.
- Affordable Housing Loans: Up to INR 35,00,000. Maximum LTV (Loan to Value): 80% for loans up to 30 Lakhs, 75% above 30 Lakhs. Tenure: Up to 240 months.
- Small Business Secured Lending: Up to INR 50,00,000 with mandatory commercial/residential collateral.

3. CREDIT SCORE & NEGATIVE PROFILES:
- Minimum Bureau Score (CIBIL): 675 for unsecured personal loans; 650 for secured housing/business loans.
- Inquiries: Maximum 3 hard credit inquiries allowed in the trailing 90 days.
- Overdue Policy: Zero tolerance for 30+ DPD (Days Past Due) in the last 6 months across existing credit facilities.
- Prohibited Industries: Speculative trading, unorganized money lenders, cryptocurrency mining ventures.
