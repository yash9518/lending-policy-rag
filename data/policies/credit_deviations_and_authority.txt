MERIDIAN RETAIL FINANCE LTD. (FICTIONAL) - CREDIT DEVIATIONS AND APPROVAL AUTHORITY (v2.1)

1. HARD STOPS (NO DEVIATION PERMITTED):
- Applicants younger than 21 years at application or older than 60 years at loan maturity are declined; age criteria cannot be deviated.
- Any 30+ DPD in the last 6 months, a written-off or settled account in the last 36 months, or an applicant reported as a wilful defaulter results in decline.
- Applicants engaged in prohibited industries (speculative trading, unorganized money lending, cryptocurrency mining) are declined.
- Loan amounts above the product maximum, and housing loans above the permitted LTV, cannot be sanctioned under any authority.
- Incomplete or unverifiable KYC is a hard stop until KYC is completed.

2. PERMITTED DEVIATIONS (REFER TO HIGHER AUTHORITY):
- Bureau score: up to 25 points below the product minimum (e.g. 650-674 for unsecured personal loans) may be approved by the Regional Credit Head with at least two compensating factors.
- FOIR: up to 5 percentage points above the applicable FOIR cap may be approved by the Regional Credit Head with at least two compensating factors.
- Hard inquiries: a fourth hard inquiry in the trailing 90 days may be approved by the Branch Credit Manager if the inquiries relate to a single loan shopping episode.
- Minimum income: up to 10% below the minimum net monthly income may be approved by the Regional Credit Head for applicants employed with listed companies or government entities.
- No more than two deviations may be combined on a single application; three or more deviations result in decline.

3. COMPENSATING FACTORS:
- Accepted compensating factors are: at least 3 years of continuous employment with the current employer; liquid savings or fixed deposits of at least 6 EMIs; an existing relationship of 24+ months with clean repayment; a co-applicant with independent income; or an LTV at least 10 percentage points below the permitted maximum.
- Compensating factors must be documented in the credit memo with supporting evidence.

4. SANCTIONING AUTHORITY BY LOAN AMOUNT:
- Branch Credit Manager: up to INR 10,00,000 for applications without deviations.
- Regional Credit Head: up to INR 25,00,000, and any application with a permitted deviation up to that amount.
- National Credit Committee: above INR 25,00,000, and any deviation case above INR 25,00,000.
- The maker-checker principle applies: the officer who sources a loan cannot sanction it.
