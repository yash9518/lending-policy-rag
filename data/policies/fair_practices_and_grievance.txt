MERIDIAN RETAIL FINANCE LTD. (FICTIONAL) - FAIR PRACTICES CODE AND GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL (v2.4)

1. LOAN APPLICATION AND SANCTION:
- Every applicant receives an acknowledgement of the loan application with the expected time for a decision.
- A Key Fact Statement (KFS) with APR, fees, penal charges and repayment schedule is provided before the loan agreement is executed.
- The sanction letter states the amount, rate, tenure and all terms, in English or the vernacular language understood by the borrower.
- Rejected applicants are informed in writing of the main reasons for rejection within 7 working days.

2. COOLING-OFF PERIOD:
- Borrowers may exit a loan during a cooling-off period of 3 days from disbursement by repaying the principal and proportionate APR, without any penalty.

3. RELEASE OF SECURITY:
- Original property documents are released within 30 days of full repayment and closure of the loan.
- For each day of delay beyond 30 days attributable to the company, the borrower is compensated at INR 5,000 per day.

4. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL:
- Complaints can be raised by phone, email, branch visit or the customer app; each complaint receives a reference number.
- The Grievance Redressal Officer must resolve complaints within 30 days.
- If a complaint is not resolved within 30 days, or the customer is not satisfied, the customer may approach the RBI Ombudsman under the Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme.
