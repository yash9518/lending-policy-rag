MERIDIAN RETAIL FINANCE LTD. (FICTIONAL) - PRICING AND CHARGES SCHEDULE (v5.3)

1. INTEREST RATE BANDS (PERSONAL LOANS, FIXED RATE):
- Bureau score 750 and above: 11.0% to 13.0% per annum.
- Bureau score 700 to 749: 13.0% to 16.0% per annum.
- Bureau score 675 to 699: 16.0% to 20.0% per annum.
- Approved deviation cases (bureau score 650 to 674): 20.0% to 22.0% per annum.

2. INTEREST RATE BANDS (HOUSING AND BUSINESS LOANS):
- Affordable housing loans are offered at floating rates linked to the External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR) plus a spread of 1.50% to 3.50% based on risk grade.
- Small business secured loans are priced at 14.0% to 18.0% per annum depending on collateral type and vintage of business.
- The spread over the benchmark cannot be increased during the loan tenure except on a change in the borrower's credit risk profile, as agreed in the loan contract.

3. FEES:
- Processing fee: up to 2.5% of the loan amount plus GST for personal loans; up to 0.5% of the loan amount plus GST, capped at INR 10,000, for housing loans; up to 1.5% plus GST for business loans.
- Cheque or NACH bounce charge: INR 500 per instance plus GST.
- All fees and the all-in Annual Percentage Rate (APR) must be disclosed in the Key Fact Statement before sanction.

4. PREPAYMENT AND FORECLOSURE:
- No foreclosure or prepayment charges apply to floating-rate loans taken by individuals for purposes other than business.
- Fixed-rate personal loans: foreclosure charge of 4% of principal outstanding if closed within 12 months of disbursement, 2% thereafter; part-prepayment of up to 25% of principal outstanding per year is free.
- Business loans: foreclosure charge of 3% of principal outstanding.

5. PENAL CHARGES:
- Delayed payment attracts penal charges of 2% per month on the overdue instalment amount; penal charges are not added to the rate of interest and are not compounded.
- The quantum and reason for penal charges must be disclosed in the loan agreement and Key Fact Statement.
